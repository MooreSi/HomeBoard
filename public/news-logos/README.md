not imply endorsement. All marks remain the property of their respective owners.
Retrieved 10 October 2026 and bundled to avoid remote image requests on the display.

ICO assets were converted to PNG. `feed.svg` is an original generic RSS icon
for custom feeds, which also show the feed title rather than an unrelated brand.
