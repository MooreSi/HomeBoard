YOUR DAYS, TOGETHER

Calendar connections

Calendar Link

Display a calendar using its read-only ICS / iCalendar subscription link. No Microsoft or Google sign-in is needed. Paste a link from Outlook, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or another calendar service.

In your calendar app, look for Publish, Share or Integrate calendar and copy the ICS / iCal subscription URL, rather than its webpage URL. Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar. Google Calendar: Settings for your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. Apple Calendar: share a calendar and copy its public subscription link.

Anyone with the link may be able to read the calendar. HomeBoard stores it privately and only displays appointments. Revoke or replace the link in your calendar service when needed.

Additional calendars & family colours

Each Calendar Link stays read-only. Choose names, colours and optional family assignments. Existing URLs are stored privately; leave the replacement field blank to keep a saved link.

READY FOR EVERY DAY

Reliability & backups

Encrypted full backup
Backups include settings, private calendar connections, tokens, family data and uploaded photos, encrypted with your passphrase. Keep the passphrase; it cannot be recovered. Server folder and iCloud originals are external sources and are not copied. The current admin password is retained during restore.
Offline display
Offline viewing keeps the last successfully loaded display and photos, with a visible offline label. Editing, login, settings and backup responses are never cached. For installation/offline support on other LAN devices, use HTTPS; browsers only enable service workers on HTTPS or localhost. Clearing the cache affects this browser only.
YOUR MOMENTS, ON ROTATION
Photos


Upload photos
Upload from your device’s photo picker. JPEG, PNG and WebP are resized and converted in your browser; export unsupported HEIC as JPEG.


Apple iCloud shared album
In Apple Photos open a Shared Album → sharing / people settings → turn on Public Website and copy its link. Paste that link here, choose Apple iCloud shared album or All connected photo sources above, then check the album. This uses the public album, without your Apple ID or password. Anyone with the link can see the shared photos. New photos are checked every five minutes.
Apple Shared Albums guide ↗
Apple Photos / folder connection
Export an album from Apple Photos as JPEG to this folder, or choose a downloaded Mac Photos library (.photoslibrary). A library uses its originals folder; cloud-only photos, edits, albums and HEIC need JPEG export. The app automatically discovers changes and never deletes folder originals. In Docker use /apple-photos and mount your Mac folder with the installer. In LXC, use a folder mounted into the container.

READY FOR THE NEXT JOURNEY
Octopus smart charging
Connect directly to Octopus Energy UK to display your Intelligent charging schedule. Home Assistant is not required.
Find your account number and API key in your Octopus account’s developer settings. Credentials stay on the HomeBoard server. Leave fields blank to keep saved credentials.
Schedules can change and are refreshed about every five minutes. Some accounts or chargers do not expose planned slots. This panel displays the schedule; it does not control charging. In the custom designer, choose Add panel → Smart charging.

AT HOME, ON ANY SCREEN
Installation & network
Choose local Node, Docker, or an unprivileged Proxmox LXC. Each setup reports the address to open on your tablet.
Run locally
npm run setup -- local
Checks Node, installs dependencies, binds to the LAN and starts the server. Linux can install a boot service with --service.
Docker
npm run setup -- docker
Builds and starts a persistent container. Use --photos /absolute/folder for Apple Photos or another folder.
Proxmox LXC
bash scripts/install-lxc.sh
Run on the Proxmox host. Select container ID, downloaded template, storage and bridge. DHCP gives the container a LAN address.
Available addresses
Use a trusted home network. Router guest isolation or centrally managed firewall policies may need your administrator. Do not forward this port to the internet.
